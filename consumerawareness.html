Consumer Awareness
Important safety information for smart buying decisions.

Be an Informed Consumer

Learn how to identify genuine products, avoid fraud, and ensure safe food choices.

Key Consumer Safety Information

1. Identify Fake Organic Labels

Always check for certification codes, government-approved marks, and printed batch numbers.

2. Check for Proper Packaging

Look for sealed, undamaged packaging and verify manufacturing & expiry details.

3. Avoid Overly Shiny Produce

Unnaturally glossy items may indicate chemical polishing or harmful preservatives.

4. Know Your Consumer Rights

You have the right to return faulty goods, ask questions, and demand proper quality.

Do’s & Don’ts for Safe Purchasing

✔ Do’s:
  • Always compare prices before buying.
  • Check product freshness and manufacturing details.
  • Buy from trusted or verified sellers.
  • Inspect packaging for tampering or damage.
✘ Don’ts:
  • Do not buy items without labels or expiry dates.
  • Avoid products with unusually low prices (may be fake).
  • Do not trust verbal claims without proof.
  • Avoid produce with artificial shine or strong chemical smell.
