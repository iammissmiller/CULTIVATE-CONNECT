Frequently asked — buyer safety

How can I tell if produce is pesticide-free?

Look for trusted certifications, ask sellers about IPM/organic practices, and follow our washing checklist.

Is "natural" the same as "organic"?

No — "organic" is a regulated label in many countries with defined standards; "natural" is a marketing term without strict rules.

How do recalls get posted?

We pull official recall notices (or accept vendor reports) and publish them in our alerts section — subscribe for push updates.
