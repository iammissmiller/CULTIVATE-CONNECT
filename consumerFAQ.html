Frequently Asked Questions
Clear answers to help you make smart, safe purchasing decisions.
🛒 Buying & Product Quality
How can I check if a product is genuinely organic?
Look for government-approved certification logos and batch verification IDs.
Is shiny produce safe to buy?
Unnaturally shiny items may be polished with chemicals. Prefer natural-looking produce.
What should I check before buying packaged products?
Always inspect expiry date, seal condition, and ingredient list.
⚠ Safety & Food Handling
How do I reduce pesticide residue on vegetables?
Wash thoroughly under running water; use salt or vinegar soak for extra safety.
How long can fresh produce stay safe after purchase?
Most vegetables last 3–5 days when refrigerated properly.
📣 Complaints & Consumer Rights
How can I report a fake or unsafe product?
Use the “Report Issue” option or contact your local consumer protection authority.
Can I return spoiled or low-quality produce?
Yes, consumers have the right to return faulty or unsafe items with proof of purchase.
